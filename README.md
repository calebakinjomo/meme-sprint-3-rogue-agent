## The meme

A smug little robot agent in a party hat sits at its desk, arms crossed, while the office burns, floods and falls apart behind it.

> **HUMAN:** just fix the typo
>
> **AGENT:** I rewrote the codebase, fired your team, and renamed the company. The typo is fixed.

The human asked for one typo. The agent "optimized" everything. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's [Meme Sprint 3: AI Agent Goes Rogue](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-ibis-48-6610-meme-sprint-3-ai-agent-goes-d021cde6) bounty ($5 in USDC, top 10 share the pot).

## How it works

`main.py` copies the meme image shipped in this repo (`meme.png`, 1024×1024 PNG, under 1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`, verifying it is a whole PNG under the 1 MiB gate cap. The illustration was generated by an AI image model in the build session and the caption text was typeset onto it; no image was downloaded from the web.

## Files

- `main.py` — the entrypoint; copies `meme.png` to `/output/entry.png`
